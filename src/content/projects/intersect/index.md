For the modern day problem solvers, strategists, city explorers, boundary conquerors.

INTERSECT

interconnectedness; inter-changeable; choices; opportunities; hope.

INTERSECT encapsulates my attitude and intention to merge and expand different systems to find many solutions to solve complex problems. By implementing the puzzle design system to elevate the wearer’s experience and intensify their connection to the garment.
