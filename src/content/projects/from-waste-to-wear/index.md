I have committed to not using virgin textiles in my practice since 2022. Instead, I adopt a material-oriented approach rather than a design-dominant one. I actively seek out deadstock textiles, collect scrap fabric, and repurpose second-hand clothing to reduce textile waste that would otherwise end up in landfills. This collection is entirely crafted from waste fabric, employing a "material-centered" design process. The patterns are meticulously adapted to fit the shapes of the fabric scraps, ensuring optimal utilization of the materials. Each scrap is thoughtfully collaged, preserving its original form to minimize additional waste. Unlike conventional practices that overlook the selvage edge, this collection intentionally embraces it as a unique and distinctive finishing detail. By repurposing discarded materials, I aim to redefine our perception of waste and showcase the potential for sustainable fashion.
